Clifftop Discovery (original synopsis, written as a short test passage - not the
published story text)

Julie and Adam drove the winding coast road back to Cornwall, ten years almost
to the day since they had first fallen in love on these cliffs. Julie leaned
against the window, watching the gorse-covered headland slide by, and thought
of the engagement ring she had lost on a walk here a decade ago - slipped from
her finger into the long grass above the cove, never found despite hours of
searching in the fading light.

What Julie didn't know was that Adam had spent the last month quietly
arranging for a pair of local metal detectorists to comb the clifftop meadow
above the cove, working early mornings before the tourists arrived, sweeping
their coils through the wildflowers and bracken.

Meanwhile, a woman named Megan was walking her dog along the same clifftop
path when the dog pulled free of its lead and bounded into the long grass,
digging at something half-buried near a cluster of sea campion. Megan knelt
down, brushed away the soil, and found a small gold ring glinting in the
morning sun.

That evening, Adam led Julie along the cliff path to the Minack Theatre,
the open-air amphitheatre carved into the rock above the crashing waves. As
the sun dropped toward the horizon, he took her hand, produced the ring that
Megan had handed in to the local pub earlier that week, and asked her to
marry him all over again.
